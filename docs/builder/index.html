1. Basics

Choose how many days a song waits in Liked Songs before rules apply, and where unmatched songs should end up (if anywhere).

Example: a threshold of 14 means a song liked today is left alone until it has sat in Liked Songs for two weeks.

Leave blank for 14.

e.g. Inbox Overflow. Blank leaves unmatched songs alone.

2. Languages

Tell SpotiSort which of your existing playlists already hold one language — it learns that language for every artist in that playlist.

Example: mapping "Chill Hindi" → Hindi means every artist who appears in that playlist is now recognised as Hindi elsewhere too.

    Off by default — a weak guess.

    logging.include_track_names — off means public logs show counts and links only, not titles.

    3. Rules

    Rules run top to bottom and the first match wins — order is priority. Put specific rules above broad ones.

    Example: a rule matching "language is Hindi" placed above a catch-all rule means Hindi songs go to your Hindi playlist even though the catch-all would also match them.

    Reorder: drag a rule by its handle, or focus the handle and use the Up/Down buttons or Alt+↑/↓.
